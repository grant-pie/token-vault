Privacy Policy

Token Vault doesn't have accounts or passwords. Access to anything you buy works through a private link sent to your email instead, so the data we collect is kept as small as we can make it.

What we collect: your email address when you buy or restore credits; the character or monster description you type into the generator; an optional name, email, and message if you use the feedback form; and your IP address, logged briefly to stop abuse of the generator and feedback form.

How long we keep it: IP logs expire automatically within about an hour and aren't stored alongside anything you submit. Your email and credit balance are kept for as long as needed to honor your purchase and let you restore it on a new device — the access link tied to it stays valid for about 400 days. You can ask us to delete your email and balance early; see Contact below.

Who we share it with: Paystack processes payments — we never see or store your card details. Resend delivers our transactional emails (receipts and restore links). The description you submit is sent to our AI image provider to render your token. Cloudflare hosts the site and provides basic, aggregate traffic analytics.

Your choices: to see, export, or delete the data we hold on you, or to stop receiving email from us, reach out through the feedback form with the email address in question.

Refund Policy

Generation credits are a digital good and don't expire. Once a credit has been spent generating or editing a token, it can't be refunded — we're charged by our AI provider the moment the image is created, so that cost can't be undone on our end.

If something went wrong with a purchase itself — you were charged but never received credits, charged twice, or charged the wrong amount — contact us through the feedback form with the email you used and we'll investigate and correct it.

Terms of Use

Tokens you generate are yours to use, print, and reuse at your table. Because they're produced by an AI model from the text you provide, you're responsible for what you type: no hateful, sexual, or illegal descriptions. We reserve the right to refuse a generation or block abuse of the generator, and credits spent on a request we refuse for this reason aren't refunded.

The ready-made art already in the Vault was produced the same way and is offered for personal, non-commercial tabletop use.
