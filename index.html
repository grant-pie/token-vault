What is the Token Vault?

The Token Vault is a free library of character and monster art for tabletop role-playing games. Every token is sized and cropped for use as a battle map marker, so you can drop a face onto the grid the moment you need one — no image editing required. Whether you run Dungeons & Dragons, Pathfinder, or your own homebrew campaign, the vault is built for game masters and players who want their table to look as good as it plays.

There are three ways to find a token here. Browse the Vault to search the existing collection by name or art style and download anything that catches your eye. Forge a Hero to describe a player character in your own words and let our AI generator conjure a matching portrait in seconds. Or Summon a Monster to do the same for beasts, villains, and other creatures your party might face. Every token you generate is yours to keep and reuse at your table.

Browsing and downloading tokens from the vault is completely free. The AI generator is different — running it costs real money on our end, so generating a custom token requires purchasing credits. Prefer not to pay? The vault already has a growing library of ready-made tokens to use instead. New tokens, styles, and features are added regularly, so check the news below for updates from the vaultkeeper.
